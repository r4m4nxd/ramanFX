## 2. Glossary (pages 1-72 only)

- **Engulf / Engulfing candle** (p4, p20): a candle whose body covers the previous candle. The drawings show it engulfing the prior body and, in some cases, the prior wick. It is used both as an entry point and as confirmation. "The best confirmation is in the Engulfing Candle Body" (p20). In the examples the zone is drawn at the engulfing candle's **open** (body edge), not its wick (p21-24, 52-56, 65-70).
- **Body candle** (p25, 45, 51, 59): a non-engulf confirmation, i.e. a solid-body candle in the trade direction on the HTF that has not reached the "end of the trend" / danger zone. The exact size criterion is not given (unclear).
- **Entry Point** (p5): one of only 4 base patterns: **RBR** (rally-base-rally), **DBD** (drop-base-drop), **RBD** (rally-base-drop), **DBR** (drop-base-rally). In the drawings the base is 1 small opposite-colour candle (p6-7) or a zig-zag sideways range (p8-9).
- **Movement** (p10-16): **Reversal** = the trend changes direction. **Continuation** = the market moves one way, up or down (drawn with a trendline under/over the swings). **Sideways** = listed only.
- **Beginning / Middle / End of the trend** (p17-18): a trend's range is divided into three. For a sell, the top is the beginning and the bottom is the end. For a buy, the bottom is the beginning and the top is the end.
- **Danger Zone** (p17-18, 28-30, 37, 40-42, 61): the **end of the trend** relative to your trade direction, i.e. the opposing demand zone for a sell or supply zone for a buy. In the setup pictures it is drawn as a box at the **origin of the prior trend**: for a sell after an uptrend, the low where the uptrend started; for a buy after a downtrend, the high where it started. Rule: the move must not have touched it. If it has, the setup is invalid or high risk.
- **Sell attempt / Buy attempt** (p17-18): an entry in the trend's direction at a pullback (lower high or higher low) in the beginning or middle portion of the trend.
- **Setup** (p19, 26): a defined pattern that gives a "precise and safe" position. Setups are "part of the Big Player's plan".
- **Confirmation / MTF** (p19-25): after finding a setup on the LTF, switch to a higher TF and look for an engulf (best) or a body candle at the same level that has not touched the end of the trend.
- **Hybrid 1** (p28-33): reversal setup = trend ends, then a retest at an RBR/DBD/QMR base, with no touch of the Danger Zone. The author calls it high risk, says it is widely used, and says "always FAST" it.
- **Hybrid 2** (p35-42): reversal setup = Hybrid 1 plus trendlines and MTF. Points 1 and 2 define a new trendline (a parallel/counter line). The swing at 2 must break the previous trendline. HTF engulf or reversal candle. Entry at the 3rd touch ("third trendline").
- **SNRC1** (p44-56): "SnR Continuation 1". Buy = Strong Resistance + RBR at the same price line. Sell = Strong Support + DBD at the same line. Entry on the retest of the line after the breakout. HTF engulf/body confirmation.
- **SNRC2** (p58-70): sibling of SNRC1. Buy = Strong Resistance + DBR. Sell = Strong Support + RBD. Price breaks the level, comes back through it to form the RBD/DBR base around the level, then leaves again. Entry on the retest. Valid only if the RBD/DBR leg did not touch the Danger Zone (p61).
- **SNRC3** (p72): "not a setup", the same as SNRC1 with its own formula (defined after p72).
- **Strong Support / Strong Resistance** (p45-70): a prior swing low or high level. It is drawn as a horizontal cyan band through candle bodies and wicks at a visible prior turning point. What makes it "strong" is **not defined** (unclear).
- **QMR, QMM, QMC, Manipulation, CLAB, Blindspot, QM2P** (p27): only named in this range, with their category. QMR is drawn on p41 as H, L, LL, HH, with the level at L.
- **"x"** in annotations (p37, 40-42, 51): means "not" (Malay "tak"), e.g. "Price x reach Danger Zone" = price did not reach it.
- **FAST** (p33): unexplained (unclear; possibly a quick, short-held trade).
- Terms in the brief that are **not present in p1-72**: QML, MPL, SBR/RBS, DMD, SISD, BSD, Road Block, Hidden Confirmation, MEF, Fresh/Unfresh, Flag limit, Compression, Storyline.

## 3. Rules (as written or shown)

**Drawing**
- Entry points are the 4 base patterns (p5-9). Base = 1+ small candles or a sideways zig-zag.
- S/R line: horizontal at a prior swing high/low. In the charts the band covers the cluster of bodies and wicks at that turn (p47-70). Whether to use the wick or the body is not stated (unclear). The RBR/DBD (SNRC1) or DBR/RBD (SNRC2) must sit "in the same line" as that S/R (p48).
- HTF confirmation zone: drawn at the engulfing candle's open/body edge (p21-24, 52-56).
- Trendlines (Hybrid 1/2): the prior-trend line through swing points 1-2-3. For Hybrid 2, a new line through the reversal's points 1 and 2 (p30, 36-42).
- Danger Zone box: at the origin (opposite extreme) of the trend being traded against (p30-32, 37, 40-42, 61).
- Candlestick charts throughout. No line chart is used in this range.

**Validity / invalidity**
- A setup is invalid if price has touched the Danger Zone (p28, 29). For SNRC2, the RBD/DBR formation must not touch it (p61). The HTF confirmation candle must not have touched any danger zone (p45, 59).
- SNRC2 sell: price must first **break** the strong support (p61).
- Hybrid 2: the 2nd trendline / point 2 must break the previous trend (p36, 39).
- SNRC1 occurs "only in continuation trend" (p44). The Hybrids are reversal setups (p27).

**Timeframe sequence**
- Find the setup on the LTF, then switch to the HTF to confirm (p19-20, 25). Shown pairs: M5 to H1 (p21, 65), M30 to H1 (p22, 66), H4 to Daily (p38), M15 base on an M30 chart (p32). No fixed TF ladder is specified.

**Entry trigger**
- Limit-style entry at the retest of the level: the S/R line plus base (SNRC1/2), the RBR/DBD/QMR base (Hybrid 1), or the 3rd trendline touch, which coincides with a horizontal zone in p39-42 (Hybrid 2). The charts show entry at the first return to the line, sometimes the 2nd (p62).

**Stop-loss / take-profit / management**
- **Not specified anywhere in p1-72.** No SL placement, TP, R:R, BE or partials are described. The charts only show price moving away from the entry.

**When NOT to trade**
- Do not sell at the end of a downtrend / demand zone and do not buy at the end of an uptrend / supply zone: "Dont be silly by selling at the demand zone area" (p17-18).
- Skip the setup if the Danger Zone has been touched (p28-29, 61).
- Hybrid 1 is high risk; the author avoids long exposure ("FAST") (p33).
- Do not enter without a setup ("don't entry blindly", p26) or without HTF confirmation (p19, 25).

## 7. Codeable vs discretionary

| Rule | Status | Sketch |
|---|---|---|
| Engulfing candle (p4) | CODEABLE | Bull engulf: close > open, prev close < prev open, body_hi >= prev body_hi, body_lo <= prev body_lo. Optional variant: also covers prev high/low (wick engulf). Zone = engulf open, optionally to the prev body extreme. |
| RBR/DBD/RBD/DBR (p5-9) | CODEABLE with parameters | leg-in = candle(s) with body/range >= k (e.g. 0.6) and range >= m*ATR. Base = 1..N candles with body < 0.5 of the leg body / small range. leg-out = same as leg-in. Direction combos give the 4 types. The base zone = base body hi/lo (or wick; the book is unclear). |
| Trend phase beginning/middle/end (p17-18) | SEMI-CODEABLE | Over the last swing leg from origin O to extreme E, position of price p = (p-O)/(E-O). For a with-trend entry, "end" = within x% of the opposing origin. The book gives no thresholds, so the thirds are an assumption. |
| Danger Zone touch invalidation (p28-29, 61) | CODEABLE once the DZ is defined | DZ = the zone around the origin swing of the prior trend (e.g. the base/candle at the swing low that started the uptrend). Invalid if any low (sell) <= DZ_top between the setup formation and entry. |
| Strong S/R (p45-70) | DISCRETIONARY (approximable) | "Strong" is undefined. Proxy: a fractal swing high/low with N-bar strength, or a level touched >= 2 times. |
| SNRC1 (p46) | CODEABLE with proxies | Buy: swing high level L. Later an RBR whose base overlaps L (within tolerance), and the leg-out closes above L. Arm a buy limit at L (or the base top). Valid while price has not reached the DZ and while trend = continuation (e.g. HH/HL structure). Sell is the mirror with DBD. |
| SNRC2 (p60-61) | CODEABLE with proxies | Sell: support L (swing low). A close below L (break). Then a rally back with a high above L forming an RBD base, and a drop with a close back below L whose low stays above the DZ. Arm a sell limit at L. Buy is the mirror with DBR. |
| HTF confirmation (p19-25) | CODEABLE | On the HTF candle(s) that span the LTF setup time, require an engulf (or a large-body candle in the direction) whose body spans L, and whose range has not reached the DZ. The body-candle size threshold is unspecified. |
| Hybrid 1 (p29-30) | MOSTLY DISCRETIONARY | Needs trendline drawing and a judgement of "trend over". Proxy: break of the swing-based trendline or a market structure break, then a first RBR/DBD base after the break, entry at the base retest, DZ = origin of the prior trend. |
| Hybrid 2 (p36-42) | DISCRETIONARY (partially codeable) | Proxy: a swing 1 extreme, a lower high 2 (sell) whose formation breaks the prior trendline, a line 1-2 extended, entry when price touches the line at 3, ideally where it meets a horizontal zone. HTF engulf at 2. Trendline fitting is subjective. |
| Entry at retest | CODEABLE | Limit order at the zone edge (line / engulf open). |
| SL / TP / management | NOT GIVEN in p1-72 | — |
| "FAST" Hybrid 1 | UNCLEAR | Not definable from the text. |
