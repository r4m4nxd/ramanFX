## 2. Glossary (as used on pp.73-142)

- **Engulfing (BUE / BEE)**: Bullish Engulfing (BUE) / Bearish Engulfing (BEE). In the schematics (75-76) this is two candles: a small candle, then an opposite-colour candle whose body extends past it. The book says RBR, DBD, RBD and DBR "are actually Engulfing" (73), so the SnD base patterns are treated as engulfing events. Engulfing is never defined numerically (for example, body vs body or wick vs wick).
- **Failed Engulfing**: an engulfing at a level that does not follow through and is later broken the other way. The schematics (75, 140) show a bearish engulfing at a high, then price eventually closes back above it. It is the *origin* of the line in SNRC3 and Blindspot (74, 140-141).
- **SNRC3**: the "war" between bearish and bullish engulfings at one line. A failed engulfing of one side is followed by a fresh engulfing of the other side at the same line. Price then breaks away and you trade the first retest of that line (73-76).
- **SOP**: standard operating procedure. "Same with SNRC1 and SNRC2" (73). Those are defined before p.73.
- **QM / QML (Quasimodo level)**: named after the hunchback cartoon. The level is the left shoulder (H for sells, L for buys) of the H-L-HH-LL pattern (89-91).
- **QMR (QM Reversal)**: for a sell, an uptrend makes H, then L, then HH, then breaks down to LL (below L). The entry is when price returns to the H level. Buys are the mirror image. It "only occurs in reversal", so it is "high risk" (89).
- **QMC (QM Continuation)**: the QM structure inside an existing trend, with a trendline. The 3rd touch of the trendline ("3rd drive") must coincide with the QM level. LL must be the 2nd drive for buys, HH the 2nd drive for sells (106-108). The best entry is "after the occurrence of reversal (Fresh)" (106). The exact meaning of that phrase is (unclear).
- **Drive (1st/2nd/3rd)**: successive trendline touches in QMC/QM2P (107-108, 120-121).
- **QM2P (QM 2 Points)**: a QMR where the head is formed by 2 swing points (1 = extreme, 2 = second, lower peak or higher trough). The trendline through points 1 and 2 is extended, and it crosses the QM level at point 3 = entry. "Always appear in reversal trend", and unlike QMC "the trendline located at the same head" (119-121).
- **QMM (QM Manipulation)**: a QMR that fails. Example: a buy-QMR at L, but price falls back through L. The failed QMR's left shoulder line becomes the entry the other way. A DBD/RBD (sell) or RBR/DBR (buy) forms at the line, and you trade its retest (129-131). The author says "so many types of QMM" exist but he enters only with an HTF engulfing (129).
- **EBD** (134, chart label only): it appears where the left chart says "RBD". It probably means Engulf-Base-Drop, but it is (unclear) and not defined.
- **Blindspot / Swap**: no conventional entry point or setup. It often appears in reversals. "Not all types of swaps can be trusted" and "price hides something in each TF" (139). Pattern: a Failed Engulfing at a line, then one large candle ("Significant Break") through the line, then a retest of the line = entry (140-142).
- **Significant Break**: a single big-bodied candle that closes far through the failed-engulfing line (140-142). It is not quantified.
- **Confirmation (HTF engulfing)**: in every "+ Confirmation" page (83-86, 99-104, 112-117, 122-127, 135-137), the LTF level coincides with an **engulfing candle on a higher TF** in the trade direction. The entry is the HTF candle's wick tagging the line.
- **Fresh**: used on p.106 only, about the QMC entry. It is not defined in this range.
- **H / L / HH / LL**: swing labels used to build the QM patterns.
- **LTF / HTF**: lower / higher timeframe. The actual TF pair is never stated.

## 3. Rules

### Drawing
- SNRC3 / Blindspot line: drawn at the **body edge of the failed engulfing**, where the two candles' bodies meet. That is the top of the bodies for a failed bearish engulfing and the bottom of the bodies for a failed bullish engulfing. The wicks poke through (75-76, 140-141, examples 77-82, 142). The schematic boxes are thin, so the "zone" is effectively a line.
- QM line: drawn at the **left shoulder (H for sell, L for buy)**. In real charts it usually sits at the body of that candle, and its wick may protrude (93, 95, 96). Some examples (92, 94) are ambiguous about whether the wick or the body is used. (unclear)
- Trendlines (QMC, QM2P): drawn on **wick extremes** of drive 1 and drive 2 and extended forward (109-111, 113-117, 122-127).
- HTF confirmation line: sometimes at the engulfing body edge and sometimes visibly inside the bodies (104 HTF, 135 HTF). (unclear) The rule seems to be: re-use the LTF price, then check that the HTF shows an engulfing there.
- No line chart is used in these pages.

### Validity / invalidity
- SNRC3 needs (a) a level that was "strong support/resistance" and became a failed engulfing, and (b) a new opposite engulfing **at the same line** (74).
- QMR: you need H, L, HH, then LL below L (sell). For a buy you need L, H, LL, then HH above H (90-91).
- QMC: you need a trendline with 3 drives. LL (buy) or HH (sell) must be the **2nd drive**. The 3rd drive must meet the QM level (107-108). It is only valid in a continuation trend (106).
- QM2P: the head must have 2 points that make a trendline, and the trendline must reach the QM level (119-121).
- QMM: the QMR must have failed (price breaks back through the left-shoulder line). A DBD/RBD or RBR/DBR must be at that line (129-131).
- Blindspot: you need a Failed Engulfing, then a Significant Break candle (140-141). There are no further validity rules, and the book warns that not all swaps can be trusted (139).
- No explicit invalidation rule (for example, "a level touched twice is dead") appears in these pages. Several examples enter on the first return, sometimes long after the setup (86, 94, 104, 136).

### Timeframe sequence
- Find the pattern on the LTF and confirm on an HTF that the same line holds an engulfing candle in the trade direction (all "+ Confirmation" pages). The author says he prefers entry with confirmation (129, 139).
- No specific TF pairs are given. The only named TFs are M30 (97, 98) and H1 (133), and those are LTF examples.

### Entry trigger
- **Limit / touch at the line on the first retest after the break-away** in every pattern (75-76, 90-91, 107-108, 120-121, 130-131, 140-141). Examples show the entry candle's wick touching the line (78, 80, 82, 92-97).
- QMC / QM2P: the entry is where the trendline extension meets the QM line, at the "3rd drive" (107-108, 120-121).
- No lower-TF trigger candle is required beyond the HTF-engulfing confirmation.

### Stop-loss
- **Not stated anywhere in pp.73-142.** The only visual hint is p.133 (AUDUSD H1): an order line labelled "sl" sits slightly *above* the buy entries. That looks like a stop moved to break-even or into profit (unclear). Pp.81 and 84 show red and green dash-dot lines above the entry with no explanation (unclear, possibly SL/TP of another trade).

### Take-profit / management
- **Not stated.** The schematics just show price continuing (to the next leg / beyond HH or LL).

### When NOT to trade
- QML is a reversal pattern, therefore "high risk", and it is also where big traders "manipulate us" (89). This motivates QMM and the confirmation requirement.
- Blindspot "has no entry point". The author prefers confirmation and warns "not all types of swaps can be trusted" (139).
- QMM: only take it with an HTF engulfing ("to be more secure", 129).

## 6. Representative examples
1. **SNRC3 buy, Example 1 (p.77)**, pair/TF not shown. A spike up leaves a *Failed Bearish Engulfing* at its top, and the cyan line is drawn at those bodies. Price drops, then grinds back up. A *Bullish Engulfing* forms exactly at the line, and price breaks above. Price later pulls back and wicks onto the line = Entry. Price then rallies to new highs. No SL/TP shown.
2. **SNRC3 sell, Example 4 (p.80)**. In a downtrend, a Failed Bullish Engulfing sits at the line. A Bearish Engulfing forms at the same line and price drops hard. Many bars later price rallies back, and a wick enters the line = Entry. Price falls again, choppy.
3. **QMR sell, Example 3 (p.94)**. H is drawn at a swing high. Price breaks above to HH and ranges above the line (touching it several times). Then one huge red candle falls to LL, below L. A sharp rally back to the H line = Entry. Price trends down afterwards. Note: the line was touched repeatedly during the HH range and still counted.
4. **QMR buy, Example 6 (p.97)**, GBPUSD M30 (23-24 Sep, year not shown). Swings L, H, LL, HH. A 10-lot buy order sits at the L line ("Buy Here"). Price wicks onto it and rallies above HH. No SL/TP visible.
5. **QMC sell, Example 1 (p.109)**. A descending trendline runs from high (1) through HH (2). The H line is cyan. Price rallies from LL to where the trendline meets the H line (3) = Entry. Price drops to below LL.
6. **QM2P + Confirmation 1, sell (p.122)**. On the LTF, a double-peak head (1, 2) gives a down trendline. The H line is cyan. After LL, price rallies to point 3 (trendline meets H line) = Entry. On the HTF, a bearish engulfing candle sits at that line, the wick retest = Entry, and a large drop follows.
7. **QMM Example 2, buy (p.133)**, AUDUSD H1. H, L, HH, then a big drop to LL (a sell-QMR set up at H). Price instead rallies back through the H line, so the QMR failed. Two 5-lot buys sit at the H line, plus an "sl" line just above them. The retest wick = Entry, followed by a strong rally.
8. **Blindspot Example 1, buy (p.142)**. A Failed (bearish) Engulfing sits at a minor high, and the line is at its bodies. Price drops, then one large green candle closes far above the line (Significant Break). A later candle wicks back to the line = Entry, and price goes up.

## 7. Codeable vs discretionary

| Rule | Verdict | Sketch |
|---|---|---|
| Engulfing detection | CODEABLE (needs a definition the book doesn't give) | bullEng[i] = close[i]>open[i] && close[i-1]<open[i-1] && close[i]>=open[i-1] && open[i]<=close[i-1] (body vs body). Choose body or wick and test both. |
| Failed engulfing | CODEABLE | After bearEng at i, with line = max(open[i], close[i-1]) (shared body top), it "fails" when a later close > line within N bars. Mirror for bulls. |
| SNRC3 | CODEABLE | Failed bearEng line L. Later a bullEng whose body straddles L (abs(open/close - L) <= tol) and closes above L. Then a limit buy at L on the first retest. Params: tol (e.g. 0.1*ATR), max bars. |
| QMR | CODEABLE | Zigzag swings (ATR or fractal based). Sell when the sequence H, L, HH>H, LL<L is confirmed. Limit sell at H (body-top variant / wick variant). Mirror for buy. |
| QMC | MOSTLY CODEABLE | Same QMR swings, plus a trendline through drive1 and drive2 (LL or HH must be drive2). Entry where TL(t) is within tol of the QM level. "Continuation trend" and "after reversal (Fresh)" need an HTF trend filter, which is discretionary. |
| QM2P | CODEABLE | The QMR head has two swing extremes p1 and p2 (p2 less extreme). TL(p1,p2) extended. Entry at the first bar where price touches the QM level while TL(t) is within tol of the QM level. |
| QMM | CODEABLE | Detect a QMR. If price closes beyond the left-shoulder line before entry/after the setup (failure), and an RBR/DBR (buy) or DBD/RBD (sell) base sits at the line, place a limit at the line in the new direction. |
| Blindspot | CODEABLE with a threshold | Failed engulfing line, then a single candle with body > k*ATR (e.g. k=1.5-2) closing through the line. Limit at the line on retest. "Significant" is undefined, so k must be fitted. Book warns of low reliability. |
| HTF engulfing confirmation | CODEABLE | Resample to the HTF. Require an HTF engulfing in the trade direction whose body contains the line (or whose body edge is within tol). |
| Line drawing (body vs wick) | DISCRETIONARY / ambiguous | The book's charts mostly use body edges. Backtest both variants. |
| SL / TP / management | NOT SPECIFIED | Must be supplied by the tester (e.g. SL beyond the HH/LL or the engulfing extreme, TP at 1:2 or the opposite swing). It cannot be attributed to the book. |
| "Strong" S/R, "Fresh", "not all swaps can be trusted", "price hides something" | DISCRETIONARY | No objective definition in these pages. |
