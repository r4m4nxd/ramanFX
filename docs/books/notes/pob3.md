## 2. Glossary (terms in this range)

- **Blindspot** (p139 context; examples p142-150). A setup with "no entry point or setup" on the obvious chart. It often appears in reversals. The author also calls it a "swap" but says "not all types of swaps can be trusted", and that "price hides something in each TF". Structure:
  1. A **Failed Engulfing**: an engulfing candle against the coming direction that does not follow through.
  2. A **Significant Break**: a large candle in the new direction that closes through the failed engulfing's level.
  3. A retrace to the failed-engulfing line, where entry is taken.
- **Failed Engulfing** (p140-150). For a buy, a small bullish candle is followed by a bearish candle that engulfs it, and price then dips a little. The line is drawn at the **top of the engulfing pair**: the bearish candle's open and body top, with the box extending to the small wick (p140, p143). For a sell, the mirror: a bullish candle engulfs a bearish one, and the line is at the **bottom** of the pair (p141, p145). Exact edge (body vs wick): the box is drawn as a thin band from the body edge to the wick tip. **(unclear which edge is the order price.)**
- **Significant Break** (p140-159). A single large-bodied candle that closes through the failed-engulfing line. It is often marked as the candle that "breaks" the level. No size threshold is given.
- **Blindspot + Confirmation** (p147-150). On the higher TF, the LTF failed-engulfing plus break shows up as **one engulfing candle**. The entry line is at that HTF engulfing candle's open, so both TFs agree. This is the "price hides something in each TF" idea.
- **Blindspot 2** (p152). Blindspot 1 plus a trendline through two prior swings. The third touch (point 3) coincides with the Blindspot line.
- **Manipulation** (p161-166). A reversal setup, "more high risk than QMR", and "valid manipulation always appears in HTF". You take an HTF engulfing candle and drop to the LTF. Inside the engulfing **body** you find a DBD (sell) or RBR (buy) base, and the entry is the return to that base.
- **Engulfing body** (p162-163). The vertical span of the HTF engulfing candle's body. The LTF base must sit inside it.
- **DBD / RBR / RBD** (used p162-184). Drop-Base-Drop, Rally-Base-Rally and Rally-Base-Drop. These are supply/demand base candles defined earlier in the book (other notes); here they are only used as entry anchors.
- **CLAB, "Confirmation Like a Boss"** (p168-174). Not a setup; it is the book's introduction to confirmation. Price makes trendline touches 1 and 2, where touch 2 is an **engulfing candle** (or a **DBD/RBR** base). Entry is at touch 3, where the trendline meets the horizontal line drawn from the engulfing or base.
- **CK1, Single Confluence** (p176-184). Strong SnR + one trendline. Formula: "Any setup + CK1". The examples show the trendline's third touch falling on a horizontal SnR level, usually the QM "L" or "H" level or a "strong support" with an RBD or DBD.
- **CK2, Double Confluence or Confirmation** (p186-200). Two trendlines plus strong SnR (it "must involve strong SnR"). Formula: "Any setup + CK2".
  - **CK2 Align** (p188-189): one line through two highs, one through two lows. They converge like a triangle, and the apex (3) is the entry on the SnR level.
  - **CK2 Cross** (p190-191, p197-200): one line through two highs (or two lows). The other line runs from a **support** point (S) to a later **resistance** point (R) for a sell, or from R to S for a buy, so it crosses the structure. Both lines meet at 3, the entry.
- **CK3, Strongest Confirmation** (p202-210). "Any setup + CK2 + Solid Engulfing", and it requires MTF. On the LTF, the CK2 apex; on the HTF, a **solid engulfing** candle whose base or open is at the same price.
- **Solid Engulfing** (p203-210). An HTF engulfing candle whose body fully covers the prior candle's body. "Solid" is not defined further **(unclear)**. In the drawings, the engulfing body is much larger than the prior body.
- **Setup names referenced** (defined elsewhere in the book): QM, QMR (QM Reversal), QMC (QM Continuation), SNRC1 and SNRC2, the "Manipulation Setup", and the H/L/HH/LL labels of the QM structure.
- **HTF / LTF.** Specific timeframe pairs are never named in this range.

## 3. Rules (as drawn; page refs)

### Drawing
- **Blindspot line:** a horizontal box at the failed engulfing's open edge, i.e. the top of the pair for a buy (p140, p142-144) and the bottom for a sell (p141, p145-146). The box spans from the body edge to the wick tip of the engulfed candle. It is extended forward until price returns.
- **Trendlines:** they need two prior touches (1, 2) and are projected forward. Touch 3 is the entry (p153-159, p169-174, p178-184).
  - In the examples, touches are drawn mostly to **wicks or highs/lows**, e.g. p157 and p180.
  - Sometimes they are drawn to candle bodies or closes (p170 runs the line to the body tops of candles 1 and 2) **(inconsistent)**.
- **CK2 Align:** connect two lower highs and two higher lows. They converge (p188-189).
- **CK2 Cross, sell:** line 1 runs through two highs. Line 2 runs from the earlier swing low (S) through a later lower high (R) that formed after price broke below S (p190, p197).
- **CK2 Cross, buy:** line 1 runs through two lows. Line 2 runs from an earlier swing high (R) to a later higher low (S) after the break above (p191, p199-200).
- **HTF engulfing for Manipulation and CK3:** use the engulfing body. The entry line is at the engulfing candle's **open** or base: the top of the body for a sell, the bottom for a buy (p164-166, p206-210).

### Validity / invalidity
- **Blindspot.** The failed engulfing must be followed by a **significant break**: a large candle that closes beyond it (p140-146). No numeric rule is given.
- **Manipulation.** It is "valid" only if it "appears in HTF" (p161). The LTF DBD/RBR must sit **inside the HTF engulfing body** (p162-163).
- **CK2.** "The confluence must involve strong SnR" (p186). What counts as "strong" is not defined in this range.
- **CK3.** It does not mean three trendlines (p202). It needs the setup, the CK2 and the HTF solid engulfing all at one price.
- **No invalidation rules, such as "price closes through the line", appear anywhere in 143-212.**

### Timeframe sequence
- **Manipulation:** HTF engulfing first, then "Switch to" LTF to find the base inside the body (p162-163).
- **Blindspot + Confirmation and CK3:** find the LTF pattern and check that the HTF shows the matching engulfing (p147-150, p204-210).
- **Specific TFs are only visible on two pages:** XAUUSD H1 on p173 and XAUUSD H4 on p197.

### Entry triggers
- All entries are **limit-style touch entries** on the return to the line or apex. A confirmation candle is never required: the drawn entry is the touch itself (p143-210).
- In the screenshots, the entry is at the line, the wick touches it, and price reverses.
- **Point 3 = entry** is the universal rule for trendline-based items (CLAB, CK1, CK2, CK3).

### Stop-loss / take-profit / management
- **Not stated anywhere in pp.143-212.**
- The only SL artefact is on p197: a dashed MT4 line labelled "#4189...431 sl" sits **below** the sell entry. For a sell, that would be a stop already moved into profit, or the line could be something else. **(unclear; do not infer a rule.)**
- No targets are drawn. The green schematic legs show only "price continues in trade direction".

### When NOT to trade
- p139 (context): "not all types of swaps can be trusted", and the author prefers entering with confirmation.
- p161: Manipulation is higher risk than QMR. This implies preferring QMR, but **no explicit "don't trade" rule is given.**

## 6. Representative chart examples

1. **p143 Blindspot Ex.2 (BUY, symbol/TF not shown).** A sell-off; a small bullish candle is engulfed by a bearish candle, which is the failed engulfing. Price dips to a low, then a very large bullish candle (the significant break) closes well above the failed-engulfing top. The cyan line is at the top of the failed engulfing. Price consolidates, wicks back down to the line (entry) and then rallies strongly about 3x the break candle's size. SL/TP not shown.
2. **p147 Blindspot + Confirmation 1 (SELL, LTF and HTF).**
   - LTF: the top area has a failed bullish engulfing; a long bearish candle breaks below. Price drops far, then rallies back to the cyan line (entry) and sells off again.
   - HTF: the same move is one big bearish engulfing candle after a run of bullish candles. The entry line is at the engulfing's open (body top). Price later wicks up to that line and fails.
3. **p159 Blindspot 2 Ex.7 (SELL).** After an uptrend top, a descending trendline runs through lower highs 1 and 2. A failed engulfing is the support line, and a bearish significant break closes below it. Price pulls back, and touch 3 of the trendline lands exactly on the line (entry). Price then trends down strongly.
4. **p164 Manipulation Ex.1 (SELL).**
   - HTF: a bullish candle is engulfed by a huge bearish candle with a long lower wick. The cyan zone is the top part of the engulfing body.
   - LTF: a DBD forms at the top of that body. Price falls, rallies twice, and the second rally reaches the DBD zone (entry). Price then drops sharply.
5. **p173 CLAB Ex.4, XAUUSD H1, 10-11 Sep (year not shown), SELL.** The trendline runs from the 10 Sep high (1) through the 11 Sep 05:00 high (2, marked DBD). Touch 3 comes at about 11 Sep 23:00 at the DBD line, where there is a live "sell 10.00" order. Price then falls toward a lower horizontal line, a possible TP **(unclear)**.
6. **p197 CK2 Ex.6, XAUUSD H4, "QM + CK2 Cross", SELL.**
   - Line A runs through highs 1 and 2. Line B runs from support S1 (a low) up through R2 (a lower high after the break).
   - The lines meet at 3 on the cyan level, where there is a live "sell 50.00" order. An "sl" dashed line sits below the entry.
   - Price fell hard after the entry.
7. **p192 CK2 Ex.1, "SNRC2 Setup + CK2", SELL (Align).**
   - A descending line runs through highs 1 and 2, and an ascending line through lows 1 and 2. They converge at 3, on the "strong support" level, which had been broken via an RBD.
   - Price retests the apex (entry) and declines to new lows.
8. **p206 CK3, "Manipulation Setup + CK2 + Solid Engulfing", BUY.**
   - LTF: the triangle lines 1-2 (highs) and 1-2 (lows) converge at 3 on the cyan base. Price touches it (entry) and rallies to new highs.
   - HTF: a bullish **solid engulfing** sits at the bottom. HTF trendlines also converge at the engulfing's base (entry), followed by large bullish candles.

## 7. Codeability assessment

| Rule | Verdict | Sketch / reason |
|---|---|---|
| Failed engulfing (buy) | **CODEABLE** | Bar i-1 bullish; bar i bearish with body_i ⊇ body_{i-1}. Within N bars (N=?) some bar j closes > max(open_i, high_{i-1}) with body_j ≥ k·ATR (k unspecified; try 1.5-2). Level L = open_i (alt: high of pair). Sell is the mirror. |
| Significant break | **CODEABLE with a parameter** | Body ≥ k·ATR(14) and close beyond L. The book gives no k, so it must be tuned. |
| Blindspot entry | **CODEABLE** | After the break, place a limit at L (buy limit for buys) and fill on the first touch. Expire after M bars or if price closes beyond the opposite side of the failed-engulfing candle. The expiry/invalidation is **NOT from the book**. |
| Blindspot + HTF confirmation | **CODEABLE** | Require an HTF engulfing whose body spans the LTF failed engulfing and break, and whose open ≈ L (within tolerance). |
| Manipulation | **CODEABLE** | HTF bullish engulfing E. On the LTF, within E's time window, detect an RBR base (rally candle, 1-3 small-body base candles, rally candle) with the base inside [open_E, close_E]. Zone = base candle range. Buy limit at the zone top. Sell is the mirror with a DBD. |
| Trendline 3rd touch (CLAB, CK1, Blindspot 2) | **Partly CODEABLE** | Choose two pivots (fractal highs or lows with lookback n). Line through them; entry when price returns to line(t) AND line(t) is within tolerance of the horizontal level. Which pivots to choose is **discretionary**: the book picks "obvious" ones, so many lines are possible. Wick vs body anchoring is inconsistent. |
| CK1 "strong SnR" | **DISCRETIONARY** | "Strong" is not defined. A proxy is the QM L/H level or a level with ≥2 reactions. |
| CK2 Align | **CODEABLE given pivots** | Upper line through two lower highs, lower line through two higher lows. Compute the intersection (apex) price and time. Entry = limit at the apex price if it is within tolerance of an SnR level, after a breakout leg from the triangle. |
| CK2 Cross | **CODEABLE given pivots** | Sell: line A through pivot highs H1 > H2. Line B from a pivot low S (before the break) to a later pivot high R (after the break below S). Intersection = entry. Needs a structure-break detector. |
| CK3 | **CODEABLE (composite)** | CK2 apex on the LTF AND an HTF engulfing whose open is within tolerance of the apex price. "Solid" means body ratio ≥ x (unspecified). |
| CLAB engulfing at touch 2 | **CODEABLE** | Pivot 2 must be an engulfing bar (or a DBD/RBR base). Level = that bar's open. Entry where the trendline meets the level. |
| SL / TP / management | **N/A: not given in this range** | Any backtest must supply its own (e.g. SL beyond the failed-engulfing extreme or the HTF engulfing's far wick; TP at the next opposing level or fixed R). Label these as user assumptions. |
| "Which setup" (QMR/QMC/SNRC) prerequisite | Defined in other page ranges | Combine with the other notes. |

**Overall.** The mechanics of this section are mostly geometric and could be codified: engulfing patterns, base patterns, trendline intersections and HTF/LTF alignment. Pivot selection and the "strong SnR" judgement are the discretionary parts. The lack of SL, TP and invalidation rules is the biggest gap for testing.
