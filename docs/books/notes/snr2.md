## 2. Glossary (as used in pp.321–639)

- **Storyline**: one directional leg between two key levels or trendlines. It has 3 parts (p335–337). It is *Classic* when started from a fresh horizontal SNR level and *Advanced* when started from a trendline (p345: "fresh daily SNR levels (Classic) or the daily trendline (Advanced)"). A *Continuation storyline* is a new storyline in the same direction that starts mid-trend (e.g. "bullish weekly classic continuation storyline started", p443).
- **Three parts / phases** (p335–336):
  - **Start**: a bounce or rejection at a key level or TL plus the breakout (BO) that "sets the direction".
  - **Middle**: HH/HL (bullish) or LH/LL (bearish); re-entries.
  - **End**: price near a major opposite level or "trendline exhaustion". This is where you take profit.
  - Text p368 says part 1 = initiation and parts 2–3 = continuation.
- **MTF alignment storyline** (p321–332): monthly, weekly and daily levels at the same price. A 1H BO starts the daily storyline, a 4H BO the weekly one and a daily BO the monthly one. A daily storyline "leads to" the weekly, which leads to the monthly.
- **Weekly storyline**: starts at a fresh weekly level or TL. The start is confirmed by a **4H** QM breakout (p338). Its middle is confirmed by 4H BOs.
- **Daily storyline**: starts at a fresh daily level or TL. The start is confirmed by a **1H** BO with a valid QM (p345).
- **Monthly storyline**: started by a daily BO (diagram p322–323).
- **QM levels (part 1)** (p368, diagram p370):
  - **Apex**: the extreme (lowest point for a bullish QM).
  - **LS (Left Shoulder)**: the level before the apex.
  - **GAP level**: a level where price "jumps between candles" during the shift. Visually it is a body-to-body gap between consecutive candles (p397; the exact construction is defined earlier in the book).
  - **SBR/RBS**: the broken level that flips. Text p368 lists "SBR" for a *bullish* QM, but p396, p399 and the diagrams 370/400 use **RBS for bullish** and **SBR for bearish**. Treat p368 as a typo.
- **C.APEX (Continuation Apex)** (p369): the pullback extreme inside a trend. Text says "highest point in a retracement" for a bullish trend, but diagram p370 points C.APEX at the pullback *lows* (higher lows) of a bullish trend (unclear; the diagram is more consistent).
- **Continuation GAP / RBS / SBR**: the gap and the flipped levels formed during parts 2–3.
- **ERS (Early Right Shoulder)** (p371): an extra mini V (bullish) or A (bearish) that forms just before the QM breakout, earlier than the normal right shoulder. With multiple ERS, the **last ERS before the BO** counts (p401).
- **QMS setups**: setups built on a QM at the start of a storyline. Primary TF: 1H QM for a daily storyline (4H if clearer), 4H QM for a weekly one (p376).
- Setup catalogue (diagrams p381–388, repeated p506–513). Definitions are read off the diagrams:
  - **SBR/RBS setup**: entry on the retest of the flipped level after the QM BO.
  - **LS setup**: entry at the left-shoulder level.
  - **APEX setup**: entry at the apex level (wick extreme).
  - **QMC**: a QM with a trendline drawn through the swing points. Entry is at the LS where the TL crosses it.
  - **Reversal TL QM Failed**: an A-shape (bearish case) or V-shape (bullish) level forms at the TL break. The QM fails and the entry is at the LS.
  - **QM-to-QM (LTF)**: a second QM forms on a lower TF at the LS. Entry is at that LS.
  - **Double Apex**: two equal highs or lows. Entry is at the LS.
  - **SNRC1**: a small candle at the SBR (marked with a blue candle). Entry is at the SBR. Defined earlier in the book; **SNRC2** is used on p486: "the first 3 candles gives the snrc 2 setup, our entry based on the fourth candle".
  - **ERS setup**: entry at the ERS.
  - **MISS setup**: the SBR where a falling TL from the apex intersects it.
  - **Double TL-SBR**: two trendlines converge at the SBR.
  - **QM-CC**: a QM whose apex is a single spike candle (blue marker). Entry is at the LS.
- **Refinement / freshest level** (p389): if the HTF QM apex has a long wick, price "isn't fully respecting" it. Drop to the fresh level on the next lower TF. Chain: 4H→1H→30M→15M; 1H→30M→15M. 15M is the final stop.
- **Dual-side rejection** (p392): bullish and bearish BOs at the same fresh level. Resolve it with the HTF direction and the HTF trendline.
- **Failed QM**: see §1, p405–408.
- **GAP QMS alignment** (p409): see the rules in §3.
- **Classic level (in the alignment rules)**: an A- or V-shape swing level on a TF.
- **Confirmation types** (p413–417):
  - **Candle Rejection**: a candle on the 4H/1H/30M/15M rejects the entry level. A buy needs a *bearish* candle and a sell a *bullish* candle.
  - **Breaking Gap**: a gap broken by an engulfing candle on the 15M or 5M. Enter on the pullback to the broken gap.
  - **Trendline Breakout**: a 5M or 1M micro-TL broken by an engulfing candle. Enter immediately.
  - **QM Confirmation**: a mini QM on the 1M or 5M. Enter on the pullback to its LS.
  - **EMA Confirmation**: an engulfing candle breaks the 5M EMA. Enter immediately. The EMA is **EMA 9, close** (p420/538 settings screenshot). For a 1M QM it is the same idea on the 1M (p525).
- **Proximity** (p432): the confirmation closest to the entry level is the best one.
- **Mini storyline** (p494): a 3-part storyline on the 4H, 1H, 30M or 15M. Its part 1 is a QMS on a lower TF and its parts 2–3 hold the continuation levels. It is fractal: the "picture inside a picture" (p498).
- **Continuation setups**: setups in parts 2–3, from mini storylines or from 4H/1H SNR levels (p494, 516).
- **SNR-level continuation setup** (p543): a 4H or 1H level that shows continuation. It "marks the start" of the 4H or 1H mini storyline.
- **Power of Four** (p589): Strategy, Psychology, Risk Management, Journaling, plus Lifestyle as the "foundation". Five items despite the name.

Not in my range: DMD, SISD, BSD, Danger Zone, Road Block, Blindspot, Hidden Confirmation, MEF, MPL, Engulf rules, Flag limit and Compression do **not** appear in pp.321–639 (they are PoB / other-range terms).

## 3. Rules (with refs)

### 3.1 Drawing
- Levels are horizontal lines at swing A/V shapes and gaps, colour-coded by TF (monthly purple, weekly red, daily green in 321–367). Trendlines are drawn through swing extremes (wicks visually) for Advanced storylines (charts p341, 348, 358). Whether lines use wick or body is not stated in this range (unclear; check the earlier chapters).
- Storyline boundaries are boxes from the start level to the end level. Daily boxes are drawn inside weekly boxes (p356–357, 362, 367).
- The QM level ladder is Apex / LS / GAP / SBR-RBS, plus ERS when present (p370, 372).

### 3.2 Timeframe sequence
- The monthly direction holds weekly storylines, which hold daily storylines (p333, 352–354).
- Weekly start = 4H QM/BO. Daily start = 1H QM/BO, or 4H if clearer (p376). Monthly start = daily BO (p322).
- In parts 2–3: weekly → 4H mini storylines only. Daily → 4H or 1H. 4H → 1H/30M/15M. 1H → 30M/15M. 30M/15M → only 5M/1M trends (p500–502).
- Mini-storyline QM TF: 4H←1H/30M/15M QM, 1H←30M/15M, 30M←5M, 15M←1M (p495).
- Step 1: identify the HTF phase. Step 2: refine on the LTF. Step 3: align (p502).
- Conflict: if the daily runs against the weekly, trade the daily parts and ignore the weekly (p568). Weekly/daily alignment = "high-confidence", conflict = pullback or warning (p353).

### 3.3 Validity / invalidity
- A QMS start needs a **fresh** level or TL plus a valid QM breakout (p338, 345). A QM without fresh-level or TL support can fail, which is the QM Failed QM setup (p406).
- Multiple ERS: only the last ERS before the BO is valid, and only with confirmation: "No confirmation? No setup." (p401–402)
- GAP QMS: a 4H gap needs alignment with the 1H gap AND the 1H classic AND the 30M classic. A 1H gap needs the 30M classic AND the 15M classic. Otherwise there is no trade (p409–410).
- Continuation SNR: a 4H level (A/V or gap) must align with 1H gap/classic AND 30M/15M A/V. A 1H level must align with 30M/15M A/V. "you shouldn't take your trade directly from the 4H level without LTF confirmation" (p516–517).
- Dual-side rejection: take the side that agrees with the HTF trend or trendline and ignore the other (p392–394).
- Noisy QM: a weak reaction at SBR/RBS means skip it and wait for LS/GAP, then APEX ("This process continues until … price finally respects the structure (APEX)", p398).
- 1H SNR continuation may not use the 4H candle rejection (p534).
- For 30M QM and below use only APEX, LS, SBR/RBS, ERS, QMC, QM-to-QM and Reversal TL QM Failed (p514).

### 3.4 Entry triggers (p414–417, 421)

| Confirmation | TF for a 4H level | TF for a 1H level | Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Candle Rejection | 4H, 1H, 30M, 15M | 1H, 30M, 15M | Right after the rejecting candle closes |
| Breaking Gap | 15M, 5M | 15M, 5M | On the pullback to the broken gap |
| TL Breakout | 5M, 1M | 5M, 1M | Right after the engulfing close beyond the TL |
| QM Confirmation | 1M, 5M | 1M, 5M | On the pullback to the mini-QM LS |
| EMA (9, close) | 5M | 5M | Right after the engulfing break of the EMA |

- **Candle rejection, ambiguity**: text p416 says a sell triggers when a bullish candle rejects the level and "closes above the entry level". Diagrams p418/536 show the bullish candle's *wick* touching or piercing the level with the body closing *below* it, and the entry arrow at the close. Follow the diagram: wick through the level, close back inside (unclear in the text).
- **Rare EMA case**: if a 5M QM forms already beyond the EMA, wait for a pullback to the 5M QM level while price stays on the EMA's side (p421).
- **Combined / multiple entries**: rejections in TF order 15M→30M→1H→4H give up to 4 entries from one level (e.g. the LS). Other combos: TL break → EMA; rejection → EMA; rejection → TL; 5M EMA + 5M gap + 5M TL together; 15M rejection → 15M gap (p423–426). The examples (p445, 457, 465, 470) show "first/second/third entry" lines.
- **Mini-storyline confirmation TFs** (p524–525):
  - 4H mini with 1H QM: rejection 1H→15M, gap 15M→5M, TL 5M→1M, QM 1M→5M, EMA 5M.
  - 4H/1H mini with 30M QM: rejection 30M→15M, the rest as above.
  - With 15M QM: rejection 15M only.
  - 30M mini with 5M QM: gap 5M, TL 5M→1M, EMA 5M (no rejection, no QM).
  - 15M mini with 1M QM: TL 1M and EMA 1M only.
- First valid QM breakout (1H or 4H) = act. Later QMs = add-ons (p379).

### 3.5 Stop-loss
- "Always use at least 30 pips as your SL … never less" (p435, 547). The examples use 3.000 on XAUUSD (= 30 "pips" at 0.1), 0.300 on USDJPY and 50 points on US30.
- Alternative SL by confirmation (p435, 548):
  - 5M QM: a few pips beyond the 5M apex.
  - 15M rejection: beyond its wick.
  - TL break: beyond the last swing before the break.
  - Breaking gap: beyond the broken gap.
  - EMA: beyond the last swing relative to the EMA break.
- If 30 pips doesn't cover the level, widen it: "Catching big RR … is not the priority" (p436).
- The SL always sits inside part 1 of the storyline (p436).

### 3.6 Take-profit
- QMS (p436–437): TP = the next fresh level or TL of the storyline's TF. A 1H/4H QM that starts a daily storyline targets the next daily level or TL. A 4H QM that starts a weekly storyline targets the next weekly level or TL.
- Alignment ladder:
  - Daily vs weekly in conflict: daily TP.
  - Aligned: weekly TP.
  - Weekly vs monthly in conflict: weekly TP.
  - Weekly and monthly aligned: monthly TP.
  - All three aligned: monthly is the final TP.
- TP = end of the 3rd part, where a reversal storyline begins. If a reversal forms early, adjust.
- Continuation (p549–550): TP = next fresh level or TL, OR a **fixed 1:2 or 1:3 RR** ("Lower timeframes can be noisy … prevents trades from reaching breakeven"), OR the end of the mini storyline (4H TP = end of the 4H mini; 1H TP = end of the 1H mini; 30M/15M = the "last stop" of the 1H/4H).
- By trader type (p583–584): intraday QMS → fixed 1:3 when the next fresh level is near. Swing QMS → hold for extended RR when the next daily level is far. Mini-storyline and SNR-level trades → 1:2 or 1:3 "get in, get out".

### 3.7 Management
- Very little explicit management. p482: "price hit BE after 7 RR … wait for another setup". This implies a move to break-even after a large run (no rule given, unclear).
- p594: "Once price moves in your favor, adjust risk accordingly—but never remove your stop-loss".
- p594 "Set and Forget": set SL/TP and walk away.
- p597: do not move the SL on hope.

### 3.8 When NOT to trade
- No trade without confirmation (p401). No trade at an unaligned gap or SNR level (p409, 516).
- Ignore the side against the HTF in a dual rejection (p394).
- At most 3 losses per day, then stop (p595). 10-minute break after each trade (p595). A break after a big win (p596). A 15-minute timer before any entry (p597). No phone (p596).
- If the setup doesn't match the rules, walk away. The "5-second rule" means a valid setup is entered after counting down (p593).
- Lifestyle: 2–3 trades/day max, a fixed 2-hour session (p626).
- Journal notes (p607–608) show the students' own "should have avoided" lessons: trading against the current TF direction, no EMA confluence, entering 30 min before NFP.

## 4. Risk / money management (p611–617, as written)

Edge assumptions: "We use 30-pip SL and aim for 1:3 RR minimum" (p612). The text says the system must survive 5–7 losses in a row. "You don't need a 90% win rate … even 50% wins keep you profitable."

**Funded accounts, a 3-phase system**:
- **Phase 1 "Survival"**: risk = Max DD ÷ 30 trades ("0.33% of balance"). Example: $100K with $10K max DD gives $333/trade. The book says "0.33 lots (Gold, 30-pip SL)". At standard gold sizing ($10 per $0.1 pip per lot) $333 over 30 pips is about **1.1 lots**, so the lot figure looks wrong. Rule: at −3% DD, cut risk by 50%.
- **Phase 2 "Progression"**: formula "Account Balance ÷ 20 trades" (= 0.5%). The $50K example uses max DD $2,500 ÷ 20 = **$250**, but that division gives $125. 0.5% of $50K is $250 and 0.8 lots is consistent with $250. Rule: after 3 wins, secure profit and move to Phase 3.
- **Phase 3 "Aggressive Pass"**: balance ÷ 10 (= 1%). Example: $200K with $20K DD gives $2,000/trade, 6.5 lots. Secure profit every 2 wins. At −5% DD, return to Phase 1.
- **Master formula**: risk/trade = (Account size × Max risk %) ÷ planned trades. Adaptive: after 2 wins, lot +20%. After 3 losses, lot −50%. "NEVER exceed 1% risk per trade."
- Summary table p617: Phase formulas use "Max Drawdown ÷ 30/20/10 trades". Lot = Risk ÷ (SL × pip value).

**Personal account "flip"**:
- **Phase 1 "Conservative"**: (Balance × 5%) ÷ 10 trades. The $500 example states "$25 per trade" and 0.08 lots, which is 5% *without* the ÷10. The formula would give $2.50. Rule: never increase size after a loss.
- **Phase 2 "Controlled Acceleration"**: (Balance × 10%) ÷ 5. The $1K example states $100/trade (= 10%, not ÷5) at 0.33 lots. Rule: increase only after 2 wins in a row.
- **Phase 3 "High-Velocity Flip"**: (Balance × 30%) ÷ 3. The $200 example says $60/trade = **30% risk per trade**, 0.2 lots. Rule: stop at −30% drawdown.

**Contradictions**:
- Personal-account examples risk 5–30% per trade, while the funded section says "NEVER exceed 1%" and lifestyle p623/627 says "1-2% risk per trade".
- The formulas and the worked numbers disagree in Phase 2 funded and in all personal phases.
- There is no "full margin" or compounding schedule beyond the above.
- Lot sizes assume gold at ~$10/pip/lot except Phase 1 funded.

**Position tool in the examples**: the "Amount: 747.75" / "Qty 83" values repeat on every XAU and USDJPY example (fixed illustrative size). US30 uses "Amount 2999.51".

## 7. Codeability per rule

| Rule | Verdict | Sketch / reason |
|---|---|---|
| Storyline 3-part segmentation | Partly codeable | Define a leg from a pivot at level L_start to the next opposite level L_end (fractal pivots or zigzag). Part 1 = from the pivot to the first BO of the prior swing (QM completion); part 2 = up to ~midway in HH/HL count; part 3 = last swing before L_end. Where "middle" ends is not defined, so you must pick a threshold. |
| Weekly start = 4H QM BO at a fresh weekly level/TL; daily = 1H | Codeable *if* the earlier definitions of "fresh" and "QM" are coded | `if price touches fresh W level (±tol) and 4H QM bullish completes (close > LS high after a lower low apex) → weekly bullish start`. |
| MTF alignment (M/W/D levels coincide) | Codeable | Cluster levels from 1M/1W/1D within a tolerance (e.g. 0.1×ATR_D). Alignment = cluster size ≥ 2 or 3. |
| TP ladder by alignment | Codeable | Direction per TF = the sign of its current storyline. TP = the next fresh level of the highest TF whose direction agrees continuously from daily up. |
| Refinement (long wick → LTF fresh level) | Partly | "Long wick" needs a threshold (e.g. wick > 50% of range or > X×ATR). Then search the 1H/30M/15M fresh level inside the 4H apex candle's wick and stop at 15M. |
| Dual-side rejection → follow HTF | Codeable | `if bull BO and bear BO at the same level within N bars → keep the side == sign(HTF storyline)`. |
| Clean vs noisy QM (SBR/RBS → LS → GAP → APEX ladder) | Partly | Place limit orders at the ordered levels. A "weak reaction" (e.g. move < 1×SL away before returning) invalidates the level and moves to the next. That is discretionary unless the threshold is fixed. |
| Last-ERS rule | Codeable | Among mini V/A pivots between the LS and the BO, take the last one. |
| GAP alignment rule | Codeable | `gap4H ∩ gap1H ≠ ∅ and ∃ 1H A/V level and 30M A/V level within the gap zone (±tol)`. 1H: 30M A/V and 15M A/V within the zone. |
| SNR continuation alignment | Codeable | The same overlap test (4H level vs 1H gap/classic and 30M/15M A/V). |
| Confirmation: candle rejection | Codeable | On TF∈{4H,1H,30M,15M} at a buy level L: low ≤ L and close > L, candle bearish (per text) → enter at close. SL = low − buffer, max(that, 30 pips). |
| Breaking gap | Partly | Needs the book's gap definition. Then: engulfing close beyond the gap, then a limit at the gap on the pullback. |
| TL breakout | Discretionary-ish | The micro-trendline drawing on 5M/1M is subjective. You could approximate it with a line through the last 2–3 pivots, then an engulfing close beyond it. |
| QM confirmation (mini QM, entry at the LS) | Codeable given a QM detector | A 1M/5M QM inside the level zone, then a limit at its LS. |
| EMA confirmation | **Fully codeable** | 5M EMA(9, close). At the level zone: an engulfing candle (body engulfs the previous body) closes across the EMA in the trade direction → market entry. SL = last swing beyond, at least 30 pips. This is the dominant entry in the examples and the student journal. |
| Proximity (closest confirmation best) | Codeable as a filter | Require \|entry − level\| ≤ k pips (e.g. ≤ 10). |
| Multiple entries (15M→30M→1H→4H rejections) | Codeable | Pyramid one unit per qualifying rejection close at the same level, with a cap of 4. |
| SL ≥ 30 pips / confirmation-based | Codeable | `SL = max(30 pips, distance to confirmation extreme + buffer)`. |
| Continuation TP fixed 1:2/1:3 | Codeable | — |
| TP = end of storyline / reversal begins | Discretionary | It needs real-time detection of a "reversal storyline". At best: exit on an opposite QM BO on the storyline's confirmation TF. |
| Daily vs weekly conflict → trade daily parts only | Codeable | Direction filter = the daily storyline sign. Restrict TP to the daily level when the signs disagree. |
| Mini-storyline nesting / "earliest mini storyline" | Partly | Requires a robust storyline detector per TF; the "first that appears" rule is codeable once one exists. |
| Setup catalogue (QMC, MISS, Double TL-SBR, SNRC1/2, QM-CC) | Mostly discretionary | They depend on drawn trendlines and on earlier-chapter definitions. Double Apex and QM-to-QM are codeable with pivot logic. |
| Psychology rules (3 losses/day, 10-min detox, 15-min timer, 2–3 trades/day) | Codeable as execution constraints | Daily loss counter, cooldown timers and a trade cap. |
| Risk phases | Codeable | A state machine (P1/P2/P3) driven by equity drawdown and win/loss streaks, risk = DD/30, /20, /10. Fix the formula and example inconsistencies before using it. Cap at 1%. |

**Practical backtest core suggested by this range**:
1. HTF filter: the weekly/daily storyline direction.
2. A level built from a 4H/1H QM level (RBS/SBR/LS/Apex/ERS) aligned with an LTF A/V level.
3. Trigger: a 5M EMA9 engulfing break near the level.
4. SL ≥ 30 pips.
5. TP: fixed 3R (continuation), or the next fresh daily/weekly level (QMS).
