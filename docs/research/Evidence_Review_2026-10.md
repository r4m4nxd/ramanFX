# Evidence review: is the top-down S&D zone method profitable? (Oct 2026)

Sources: our own research (docs/research/*), a web search, and an X/web search by Grok (owner ran the prompt; pasted result summarised here).

## Verdict
Weak evidence. No third-party verified live track record was found for the method or any of the four mentors. Our own tests of the mechanical zone rules (36k zones 2023-25, ML benchmark, bot runs) were negative after realistic close-stop losses. The discretionary Raja method (HTF direction, confirmation on tested zones, runners) is untested, not disproven.

## Academic / base rates
