# What the owner's marks teach (living notes)

Source: the owner's "Train my eye" marks (table rfx_training_marks) + their annotated TradingView charts. Each item
cites the chart it came from. Update as more charts are marked; the engine is tuned to these.

## Breaks (confirmed, AUDUSD W1/D1/H4, 2026-10-30)
- A line is broken by the FIRST candle that CLOSES beyond it. The owner's break marks match the first close exactly
  (W1 up lines broke at the weeks of Mar 21 and Jun 13 2026; D1 down line Sep 21→Oct 14 broke Oct 20).

## Trend line vs momentum line (AUDUSD MN, owner-labelled)
- MOMENTUM line: drawn from the EXTREME of a leg (the top or the bottom) along the very next bars of the same one-way
  impulse (anchor 2 is a bar 1-4 bars later, NOT a real pivot), steep. MN: top Apr 2022 0.76607 → Aug 2022 high
  0.71362 (broken Nov 2022); bottom Oct 2022 0.61695 → Nov 2022 low 0.62717 (broken Mar 2023). Its break ends the
  impulse, not the trend.
- TREND line: both anchors are REAL pivots with a proper correction between them (a lower high that led to a lower
  low, a higher low that led to a higher high). MN examples: Feb 2023 0.71571 → Jul 2023 0.68944 (down, broke Dec 2023);
  Oct 2023 0.62698 → Aug 2024 0.63478 (up, broke Dec 2024); Apr 2025 0.5913 → Nov 2025 0.64206 (up, broke Jun 2026).
- The extreme CAN be pivot 1 of a trend line when the next anchor is a real pivot after a proper swing (MN Apr 2025
  bottom → Nov 2025 higher low, with the Sep 2025 high in between). It is NOT used when the move right after it is
  just the impulse / a tiny correction (gold D1: top Aug 25 skipped, line from the first lower high Sep 3 → Sep 18).

## Chaining (AUDUSD W1)
- When an up line breaks but the structure keeps making higher lows, the NEXT line starts at the previous line's
  pivot 2: W1 Nov 22 2025 → Jan 17 2026 (broke Mar 21), then Jan 17 2026 → Mar 28 2026 (broke Jun 13).
- Down line after a higher high: starts at the first lower high right after the top (W1: May 9 2026 0.72711, the week
  after the May 2 top 0.7277) → May 30 0.71896, broke Jul 25.

## Clean anchors
- Pivot 1 may sit one bar away from the exact extreme so the line touches without cutting (W1: Nov 22 low instead of
  the Nov 15 swing low).
- Pivot 2 is where the line touches while resting above (down) / below (up) the swings in between (AUDUSD MN/D1/H4, gold W1).

## Structure calls
- W1 AUDUSD: HH (May) → HL (Jun) → LH (Sep) = sideways ("no HH or LL forming").

## Real pivots = the end of a REAL correction (owner, 2026-10-30)
- After a fast momentum move, the real correction comes back NEAR THE ZONE THAT STARTED THE MOMENTUM, or about HALFWAY
  (≈50%) of the impulse. It can come right away, or after a base / sideways section first.
- The END of that correction is the real peak (in a down move) / valley (in an up move) — the actual pivot of the trend
  line. Small pullbacks inside the impulse are NOT pivots.
- The momentum line can be drawn right away, from the extreme along the impulse; the trend line waits for real pivots.

## Pivots come from REVERSAL zones (owner, 2026-10-30)
- Rally-Base-Drop (RBD) = the actual PEAK. Drop-Base-Rally (DBR) = the proper VALLEY.
- Rally-Base-Rally (RBR) and Drop-Base-Drop (DBD) are CP levels (continuation zones the market creates on the way).
- With RBR / DBD (continuation) the MOMENTUM lines happen; with RBD / DBR (reversal) the TREND lines happen.
- So trend-line anchors = RBD peaks (down lines) / DBR valleys (up lines); momentum lines run along RBR / DBD legs.

## Fractal structure: impulse vs correction across timeframes (owner + Raja, 2026-10-30)
- The HTF keeps making HH/HL (or LH/LL). Its CORRECTIONS, seen on the LTF, are themselves a full opposite trend
  (LL/LH inside an HTF uptrend's pullback), while on the HTF they look like a sideways pause. Structure nests like
  cells → atoms → particles: read top-down.
- The biggest picture gives the direction and tells whether the HTF is in an IMPULSE or a CORRECTION right now.
- Trade WITH the HTF impulse. Correction trades are allowed only on the LTF of that HTF, and only until price reaches
  the HTF trend line or the HTF zone near the trend line (where the HTF impulse is expected to resume).
- Engine implication: classify each TF's current leg as impulse / correction relative to its HTF; LTF trend lines
  inside an HTF correction point against the HTF trend and end at the HTF line/zone.
