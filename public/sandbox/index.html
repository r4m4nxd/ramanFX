Why no head-and-shoulders, flags or triangles?

Kevin, Seiden, Raja and Baker read where unfilled orders sit, not shapes. A head-and-shoulders “right shoulder” is only worth trading if it happens to be a fresh rally-base-drop supply with room below; the shape itself carries no edge, and the crowd that trades the pattern is usually the liquidity the supply-and-demand trader sells to. So the drill uses the structures they actually trade: the four zone types, gaps, traps, retests and level quality.
