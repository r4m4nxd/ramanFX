## Rules and lessons

- Trendline drawing: if attaching to the previous valley gives a near-horizontal line, attach from the lowest point to the next valley instead.
- A zone hit before it qualified is not a zone.
- Do not draw the basing bigger than it is, even if the real basing spans more candles.
- A rally-base-rally (even unqualified) is a good place to hide your stop on a runner.
- Set-and-forget zones just before a higher-timeframe zone need a wider stop for wiggle room.
- If liquidity is targeted without the zone being hit, the zone is more likely to fail.
- In a daily downtrend, trade with that trend - counter zones fail more when overextended.
- Removal of a proven zone plus a trendline is a strong signal in the other direction.
