## Rules and lessons

- A set-and-forget zone that becomes part of a daily zone needs a wider stop.
- Near-miss on a daily zone: move the zone so it counts as hit (conservative), then look for confirmation.
- Confirmation inside a daily zone needs an obvious explosive move away that breaks no zones.
- Use the channel tool: at the bottom of a down-channel, wait for a pullback before shorting.
- As price approaches a higher-timeframe opposing zone, tighten the stop and lock in gains.
- Better-looking zone vs better-located zone: manage the first aggressively, the second less so.
