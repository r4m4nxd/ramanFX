## Rules and lessons

- Zones created by a market-wide news spike (every pair makes the same move) usually fail; a spike in only one pair is more trustworthy.
- Old zones are preferred with a liquidity trap in front - not required, but without it he does not love them.
- When a lower-timeframe zone sits just before a higher-timeframe zone, the higher-odds play is to wait for the HTF zone and take LTF confirmation.
- If you set-and-forget the nested LTF zone, widen the stop into the HTF zone (about halfway) and take 1:1 if price only wicks.
- Prefer the higher-timeframe zone when both exist (focus on 4H over 1H).
- Do not draw trendlines from awkward wicks unless the peak is still good without the wick.
- A demand far below the old high (away from support/resistance traders) is a good location.
