## Rules and lessons

- Watch the Sunday sessions to learn - do not just follow the breakdowns.
- Let a percentage of a winner ride to the long-term target rather than holding full risk to it with no trend.
- Old zones without a liquidity trap often only give a small move - manage to break-even.
- When the weekly trend and daily zone are in control, do not cap the trade at 1:1 - trail it (2:1, 3:1 or more).
- Trail a runner along the trendline but with wiggle room for fake-outs.
- Most trades: aggressive management, slowly growing 1-2% a month; 5-10 big runners a year boost the account.
- Counter-trend setups are allowed if managed aggressively - this changed his results more than being picky.
- A zone at an opposing high needs a wider stop.
- Compare moves away: trade zones whose move away is clearly strong; mediocre zones need confirmation or aggressive management.
