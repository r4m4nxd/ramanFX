## Rules and lessons

- Watch the Sunday meetings to learn how to identify zones - the daily breakdowns alone are not enough.
- A 4H supply that forms off the weekly supply is the weekly supply's confirmation.
- Draw the trend line from the true peak; a line that is too horizontal is wrong.
- An old 1H zone with a 4H zone sitting just below gets aggressive trade management.
- Trend lines are day-trading guides: 1H trend line -> 5m entries, 4H trend line -> 15m entries.
- A set-and-forget zone above liquidity needs a wider stop.
- No higher-timeframe trend or zone in control: aggressive trade management.
- To turn an aggressive trade into a bigger R:R: stop at break-even (or half risk), take-profit further out with an alert at 1:1; when the alert fires lock in about 0.75R and keep trailing the stop with price.
- He rarely draws two zones, but two stacked daily supplies can both be waited on for 1H confirmation.
- With the daily trend a larger R:R makes sense, but use hybrid management when price sits at the daily high.
- A counter-trend long at a tested demand while overextended: aggressive management, bump the stop up if it gives another leg.
- A wick into a supply zone: take confirmation on day-trading timeframes instead of set-and-forget.
- A garbage chart is not traded.
