frames in `public/lessons/raja_hi/`. Where a topic was already covered by an existing lesson, the new points were merged into it,
tagged with their source, e.g. "(course 3)", "(live session, Feb 10)", "(Apr 2025 class)", "(2022 meeting)". No lesson was renumbered.
Points the notes marked unclear were dropped. "(likely)" was kept where the reading is uncertain. Account logins and other personal details were left out.
