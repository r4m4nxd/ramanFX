# Raja course 1 - Complete basic (44 min, recorded ~2020-03-26 per chart clock) - timestamped outline

- 00:00 Intro: ~8 years on S&D, 5-6 years and ~10-12 other methods before. S&D is accurate because market makers use it; retail uses Elliott wave, Gann, price action etc.
- 00:44 Follow daily, weekly, monthly - market makers don't look below daily. Practise HTF first to read direction, then take entries on LTF (even M5).
- 01:28-02:36 Milk-shop analogy: demand > supply -> price up (you pay double for 3 packets); excess supply about to expire -> sold cheap, price drops.
- 02:36 Market moves between HTF (monthly) supply and demand: supply's target is demand and vice versa; LTF valleys/peaks and trades happen in between.
- 03:15-06:01 Rectangle box: supply on top, demand at bottom. Proximal line = limit entry (buy limit at demand top, sell limit at supply bottom); distal line = stop loss; TP = proximal line of the opposite zone. Never sell into a falling market "on execution", even a 1,000-pip day - wait for price to reach the zone. Trade only with a zone, wait even a month. Follow pips not money; same rules for 10 USD or 100k accounts, small size, grow the account.
- 06:01 Candles: big = departure candles (any big/engulfing); small = base candle (like a wall's foundation). Base works as stopper and in continuation patterns.
- 07:00-08:40 Rules: departure must travel at least 2x the base-candle area away. Demand zone = bottom wick + thick body, ignore top wick (supply: top wick + body). No departure = no zone. Wait for the departure candle to close (beyond 50% of base area) before drawing.
- 08:44-09:22 Market often tests only the thick body; sometimes runs to the edge (distal) and reverses - hold the position until the distal line is crossed, then it is a stop/close at loss; a broken zone drops a lot.
- 09:28 Course topic 1: two types of S&D. (a) Valleys and peaks - V shape (demand: drop-base-rally) and inverted V (supply: rally-base-drop); "Kashmir valley / Hindu Kush peak" analogy. (b) CP = continuation pattern, and PCP (likely "potential continuation pattern") levels.
- 11:04-12:20 Leg-in (leg 1) candles must be in/near the base area, leg-out (leg 2) leaves it. Ignore far wicks on LTF.
- 12:21-13:50 Chart (AUDUSD monthly): every TF, even M1, has its own valleys/peaks. Marks fresh zone, CP levels, PCP level, retests; an area "where we can't sell, it's a buy area" though still technically weak.
- 13:50-15:06 CP: drop-base-drop / rally-base-rally. Bus-station analogy - stop to refuel on the way to the destination. CP = trend continues. After 3 CPs don't trade the next CP; mostly market retraces/reverses.
- 15:50-18:40 Why it works: market makers (banks, big firms, millions+) vs retail (1 USD or 100k, still retail). Makers create fake signals: retail sells on LTF, makers buy, retail stops out, revenge-buys, makers drop it again -> CP forms; hunting retail. Trade with market makers, not against.
- 18:45-20:25 PCP: a CP (or just a departure) that removes the opposite zone = PCP; any CP formed after removing the opposite zone is also PCP. Plain CP = zones formed on the way to the destination.
- 20:30-22:17 Base candle count: 1-6 candles inside the box range is tradable; more than 6 = weak zone. Stair-stepping 3 candles (open above/close above) = weak; 1-2 fine.
- 22:17-25:21 Base candle shapes: hammer (common), marubozu (no wick, strong imbalance - more later), doji (slab-like, weak, wick gets filled - don't trade a single doji), ERC = extended range candle (fills departure area, can still draw), a candle he calls by a rude word with no upper wick (likely Japanese name), engulfing - draw supply with top wick + thick body. Drawn on a EURUSD monthly frame (25:59).
- 25:24-28:20 Gaps: weekend news (e.g. US attacks Iran) -> Monday open gap. Gaps always get filled, even after a year; use the zone before the gap, not the gap zone. Bitcoin weekly examples: bullish/bearish gaps filled. BTC at ~6,000 now, gave students long entry; long-term target ~60-64k (likely). Entries can take 4-5 weeks.
- 28:28 Q (student, English): price sitting between top supply and bottom demand - what then? Answer mostly unintelligible (29:00-29:40 garbled ASR); 29:45 resumes: BTC demand + CP level, trendline heading to top supply; enter on H1/H4/M1 inside.
- 30:15-31:35 Zone types: 1) fresh, 2) original, 3) retested. Prefer fresh and original; retested OK if following monthly, depends on zone. 4th: multiple zones (not in the course).
- 31:36-32:20 Fresh = drop, base, reverse, not revisited (check right side). Retested = revisited. Prefer fresh.
- 32:21-34:20 Multiple zones ("hierarchy"): zone 1 strong/retested; zone 2 formed because of zone 1's impact; zone 3 impacted by zone 2 (fresh, not retested); zone 4 formed by itself, no prior zone behind = original. Trade fresh and original.
- 34:36-35:52 Can't trade every zone - strong zones only, weak not; strong/weak is next session. S&D works with the trend: trade fresh zones along the trendline; trendline crossed -> bearish becomes bullish, start buying. No trades after 3 CPs.
- 35:53-37:55 Chart demo (BTC monthly, then EURUSD monthly): top fresh zone retested; zone 2 fresh but from zone 1's impact; lower 2-candle CP made by itself = original (now retested); also a PCP (removed a demand level). Retested zones usable on monthly/daily but strong zones preferred.
