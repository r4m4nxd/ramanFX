- 00:00 Topics: how the trendline changes, and high curve / low curve.
- 00:05 Recap arrival zone: the first zone that both crossed the trendline and removed the opposite zone (strong zone). HTF strong zones (monthly/weekly/daily); on LTF you keep finding new ones.
- 00:52 Market does not have to hit the opposite zone - it can drop before it (high curve, next topic).
- 01:05 Rule: one opposite zone removed + trendline crossed = OK, still the same trend. When the market removes the second opposite zone too (and has crossed the trendline) the trendline changes: bullish ends, bearish starts. You can no longer use the bullish trendline.
- 01:45 Where does the new trend run to? To the opposite arrival zone of the HTF.
- 02:26-02:54 HTF chart example: last demand and second demand both removed -> trendline totally changed, now pointing down.
- 02:58-04:24 Gold (cleaner for this): daily - zone removed, trendline almost crossed, reversed at the weekly zone. Weekly: trendline crossed + opposite zone removed = arrival zone; first supply broken, then the second -> trendline changed, new (bullish) trendline started. Another spot the same: trendline crossed, one zone removed, then second.
- 04:24-06:00 His live gold position: trendline crossed, one zone (single candle) broken, retested once, twice; he expected it to break on 3rd/4th hit, sent the analysis to students and posted on Instagram. Expects ~5,000 pips profit (likely). Bearish trendline finished, bullish started; market went far up.
- 06:03 Q (student): should this be seen on the entry TF (daily / weekly)? A: each TF on its own - monthly trend changes when two monthly opposite zones (e.g. two supplies) are broken; daily separately.
- 06:43-07:22 Drawing: first supply, second supply; the zone that crossed the trendline + removed the opposite zone = arrival zone. After crossing, price goes to hit it, may drop from there; if it removes it, can run 2,000-5,000 pips.
- 07:30-08:17 USDJPY weekly (likely; frame at 07:35): two opposite zones removed + trendline crossed -> bearish trendline started, bullish finished. Daily too: demands removed + trendline crossed -> bearish. This is a rule, on any pair and any TF.
- 08:21-09:03 Which TF / which arrival zone to prefer when there are 2-3 (e.g. H4 removes two opposite zones - where does it stop?) - covered later in the realignment topic.
- 09:07 Two supply zones removed -> that is the arrival zone; same mirrored for demand.
- 09:31-10:17 Valid trendline cross: not one candle poking through and reversing. Need a proper departure candle closing beyond the trendline, and the next candle also closing beyond (even if small). One candle crossing then closing back = not a change.
- 10:17 Topic 2: high curve / low curve. Take two strong zones (supply above, demand below). Put the 50% line between them (Fibonacci), plus 0-25% lines at each end.
- 10:47-11:44 Lower 0-25% (low curve) = for buyers; you can buy up to 50%, even 75% area (likely). Upper 0-25% (high curve) = for sellers, not buyers: a demand found there can't be used - market can drop any time, it need not reach the supply. Market there is overbought / oversold; don't trade against it.
- 11:53-13:22 Gold monthly (frame at 12:22): supply retested four times, then broken; demand below. 50% and 0-25% between them. On LTF you'll see it: market dropped into the 0-50% / low curve, entered there and went straight up and broke the supply. Low curve took buyers, high curve was the sellers' area.
- 13:22-14:44 Gold now: strong zone + opposite zone, 0-25% drawn; price is in the high curve = sellers' area. Zoom out on daily to see. A new demand here can't be bought. Wait: either the opposite zone gets removed, the trendline changes, price reaches the supply, or a fresh supply forms to sell; or use a confirmation order. Market already dropped from there once and is retesting.
- 14:49-15:30 Will update on good gold trades. Still holding his gold buy (bought after daily trendline cross), SL at the last level; TP still above. Latest high curve = sellers, low curve = buyers.
- 15:36-16:09 Since price is in the high curve, even on LTF no fresh daily buy; that zone near 50% has no proper departure candle and removed no opposite zone. Wait for the market to remove/cross the supply, then place buy limit at the new area.
- 16:09-16:58 HTF supply = high curve; a daily or weekly (LTF) demand forming right under it is not preferred - sellers / market makers sit at HTF zones and can cross it any time. If the HTF zone has been hit once or twice, no buy entry; let LTF remove the HTF zone, then the LTF zone becomes strong and usable.
- 17:05 Summary: trendline changes when the market removes one opposite zone and then the second.
- 17:20-18:08 Why high curve matters: most S&D traders lose here. When the trendline is crossed and the second zone removed, don't wait hoping for reversal - close the position at whatever loss; trendline changed, reversal from the opposite zone is not guaranteed, news can remove it and the whole account is lost.
