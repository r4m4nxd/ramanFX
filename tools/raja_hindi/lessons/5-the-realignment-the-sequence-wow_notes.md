Meaning translated, not word for word. (likely) = uncertain reading. Check against the video. Charts: MT4 (Pepperstone), mostly GBPJPY monthly, EURUSD daily/H4.

- 00:00 Small talk on a live trade (student has the same view); HTF trend still says up.
- 00:30 Topic: realignment, with two parts. 1) Sequence number: read direction on HTF (monthly + weekly up) and trade the same way on daily/H4/H1 (both down -> sell on daily). Follow the HTF.
- 01:10 2) Realignment: monthly + weekly going up but daily has already run up -> daily reverses and re-aligns with the HTF; find the daily (or weekly) demand the market retraces to, enter there and go with the HTF.
- 01:55 Best entry: all three TFs line up -> nested area (like location area from the location/momentum lesson). Here different: the LTF reverses to reach an HTF zone.
- 02:24-03:12 Drawing: sold from an H4 location area, target the demand level; weekly dropping with a CP level, daily dropping.
- 03:23 When daily/H4 crosses its trendline it has lost momentum -> use the next HTF zone (daily/weekly) and wait for the market to go there = realign. Then trade the LTF from that zone.
- 04:46-07:28 EURUSD daily/weekly: if daily crosses the trendline and removes the opposite zone, it retests the zone above. Trendline drawn through two higher highs; where the trendline changes = weekly realignment area. Daily lost momentum -> checks the weekly; that was a weekly CP level.
- 07:28 Sell from there: fresh zone retested twice, then entry and drop. Daily re-aligned with weekly. Monthly + weekly dropping, daily already dropped a lot.
- 08:13-09:06 If the market removes this zone too it looks up for the next opposite zone; weekly area (3 touches) = secondary alignment area (likely); breaking it opens the upper zone.
- 09:22-10:20 The upper zone is actually monthly (a monthly CP level). Rule: whenever the LTF crosses its trendline it goes to the HTF realignment area.
- 10:24 If it drops from here, buy from the lower area.
- 10:32 Q (student): sold this on daily (H1 confirmation entry) and got stopped out. A: that sell (first drop / left shoulder) was not valid - it sat inside a demand level (first rule from earlier). Mistake; avoid next time.
- 12:02 EURUSD H4 (frame 11:31): this zone valid but weaker departure candle; it removed the opposite zone -> future sell zone if market drops.
- 12:43 Example: H4 zone, H1 drops and crosses trendline -> use the H4 zone that lines up with realignment, is strong and removed an HTF opposite zone; sell when momentum is lost. Daily lines up with it too.
- 13:57 Must follow sequence number; trade the same way on H1/M5.
- 14:08-15:12 HTF near bottom, trendline just crossed; two trendlines; technically a buy from here, HTF shows long (opposite zone removed + trendline crossed) -> target upper zone.
- 15:17 Don't over-draw trendlines; each trader uses them their own way, use what is easy.
- 15:39 Realigning; when momentum is lost market easily reaches the area -> then sell. End of realignment.
- 16:03 WOW trade (16:11-16:35 aside, answering a message).
- 16:39 WOW: supply 1 above, supply 2 below. Demand breaks supply 2 -> either retrace/enter then go up, or go straight up; target = supply 1 either way. HTF opposite zone removed -> second opposite zone is the target; pips in between = extension of pips; on LTF many peaks/entries along the way.
- 18:03 One zone breaks -> target is always the next zone.
- 18:09 Q (student): entry? A: LTF (daily/H4) normal entries; set and forget or confirmation, depends.
- 18:32 Gap between supply and demand = WOW trade gap.
- 18:40-20:42 Risk management: profit margin at least 3x stop loss (2x minimum). If supply and demand are close (1x) don't trade - sideways chop; wait for a zone to break. Applies H1/H4/daily/monthly.
- 20:42-21:44 Two WOW areas: break of first zone -> first area (smaller), then second (bigger) after the next zone goes. Good trader: max two positions on a zone; close one at TP, hold the other for the zone removal to catch the extension (~1,000 pips (likely)).
- 21:49 EURUSD daily: big black candle closes through the trendline -> trendline changes; demand was weak, but black candle to demand ~300+ pips = WOW area 1 (always smaller).
- 22:50 Sellers TP one part, hold rest; next zone below: ~1,100 pips + 300 -> ~1,400-1,500 pips total. If that is removed, next zone becomes buy area.
- 24:09-25:23 LTF same: when market crosses trendline (join two trendlines), from the break to the demand level = WOW area, even if just 10 pips; EURUSD H4 ascending trendline crossed -> sell. Don't trade counter moves (impulse 20-50 pips vs counter ~10 pips).
- 25:29 Rule: market breaks one zone then another -> trendline changes on that TF. Impulse legs run longer, corrections shorter; market need not reverse at once.
- 26:20 Q (student): monthly/location area with several daily zones - which one? A: the strongest: best departure candles + removed opposite zone. Usually for supply prefer the lower daily zone, for demand the upper one - unless its departure candle is weak, then use the other.
- 27:34 Retested zones: location-area zones are often already retested (weekly tested, third time broke). Prefer fresh; on a retested zone use the confirmation rule.
- 28:35 Two opposite zones broken -> trendline changes; momentum-wise it stops at the realignment/nested area. If two opposite zones removed on the HTF trendline -> it hits the arrival zone. Mostly the realignment area is also the arrival zone, esp. H1/H4 into a daily strong zone.
- 29:38 EURUSD (frame 29:23): this zone again a sell area, retested -> use confirmation; trendline properly crossed, two opposite zones removed; retracing to make a peak.
